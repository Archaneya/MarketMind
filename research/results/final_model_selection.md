# MarketMind — ML Model Selection Evidence

## Research question
Which unsupervised clustering algorithm best recovers the simulator's market regimes from the seven engineered market features?

## Experimental protocol
- Seeds: 7, 21, 42, 100, 2026
- Simulated duration per seed: 14,000 minutes
- Analysis timeframe: 3-minute bars
- Chronological split: 70% train / 30% test
- Features: ret_5, roll_vol, rsi, ma_diff, momentum, atr_pct, volume_change
- Preprocessing: log transform roll_vol and atr_pct; StandardScaler fitted on train only.
- Ground-truth simulator regimes are used only for evaluation, never as model inputs.
- Primary selection metric: mean test ARI across seeds; mean test NMI is tie-breaker.

## Mean results across seeds

| Model | Test ARI mean ± SD | Test NMI mean ± SD | Silhouette | Davies-Bouldin | Noise % | Fit seconds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gaussian Mixture | 0.573 ± 0.068 | 0.593 ± 0.059 | 0.308 | 2.235 | 0.000 | 0.369 |
| Agglomerative | 0.539 ± 0.120 | 0.552 ± 0.074 | 0.330 | 1.228 | 0.000 | 0.221 |
| K-Means | 0.516 ± 0.103 | 0.545 ± 0.086 | 0.332 | 1.197 | 0.000 | 0.025 |
| DBSCAN | 0.008 ± 0.006 | 0.047 ± 0.007 | 0.283 | 0.846 | 5.108 | 0.122 |

## Selection decision
Select Gaussian Mixture over the K-Means baseline: it achieved the highest mean held-out ARI, with NMI as the tie-breaker. MarketMind's current prototype production detector is therefore set to Gaussian Mixture. This choice is provisional: the evidence comes from synthetic simulator data and must be re-tested on real historical market data before any live-market claim or deployment decision.

## Fair-comparison notes
- K-Means and GMM use native out-of-sample prediction.
- Agglomerative has no native predict method; test labels are assigned using the nearest centroid computed from its training clusters. This is an explicit proxy, not a native Agglomerative prediction.
- DBSCAN has no native predict method; a test point inherits the label of its nearest training core point only when within eps; otherwise it is noise (-1).
- Silhouette, Davies-Bouldin and Calinski-Harabasz are internal geometric metrics. For DBSCAN, noise points are excluded from these three metrics and noise coverage is reported separately.
- ARI and NMI compare the predicted cluster partition with simulator truth and are label-permutation invariant.

## Why this model was selected
GMM is selected because this project's primary objective is agreement with the simulator's known regime states, and GMM achieved the best mean held-out ARI and NMI. GMM also models component covariance and overlapping distributions. It was not best on every metric: K-Means had a higher mean Silhouette score, lower Davies-Bouldin score and substantially faster fit time. The selection prioritises external regime recovery over compactness and speed for this prototype.

## Limitations
- This dataset is synthetic, generated by MarketMind's own regime-switching simulator.
- Results do not establish real NIFTY regime-detection accuracy or trading profitability.
- Cluster count is fixed at four for K-Means, GMM and Agglomerative because the application defines four target regimes; DBSCAN determines its cluster count.
- The model-selection rule is an experimental choice, not proof of universal superiority.
